    Clear out the junk

    Opening good stuff helps. Telling each app what you don't want helps faster. Takes about two minutes per app.

    YouTube
    1. On any junk video, tap ⋮ → Not interested, or Don't recommend channel.
    2. You → Settings → Manage all history → delete the stretch you want gone.
    3. While training, like or save at least one video per search. Watching 30+ seconds counts more than a tap.
    Instagram
    1. Settings → Content preferences → Reset suggested content.
    2. On Explore or Reels, tap ⋯ → Not interested.
    3. Follow two or three accounts from the queue. Follows outweigh visits.
    TikTok
    1. Settings → Content preferences → Refresh your For You feed.
    2. Long-press any junk video → Not interested.
    3. Same page: Filter video keywords to block topics outright.
    X
    1. On a bad post, tap ⋯ → Not interested in this post.
    2. Settings → Privacy and safety → Content you see → Interests; untick what you don't want.
    3. Mute words you're sick of under Mute and block → Muted words.
    Facebook
    1. On a post, tap ⋯ → Show less or Hide post.
    2. Menu → Settings → Feeds to snooze or unfollow pages.
    Ads everywhere
    1. Google: My Ad Center → turn off topics you don't want.
    2. Meta (Facebook + Instagram): Ad preferences → Ad topics → See less.

    Your progress stays on this phone only. Nothing is sent anywhere.
